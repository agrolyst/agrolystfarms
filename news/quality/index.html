Quality and safety are part of building food trust

Good food handling practices, cleanliness and clear responsibilities are important at each stage of food preparation and distribution.

Good food handling practices, cleanliness and clear responsibilities are important at each stage of food preparation and distribution.

Food safety depends on the product, process and applicable requirements. Businesses should follow current local regulations and authoritative, product-specific guidance rather than relying on general advice alone. Keep relevant cleaning and handling procedures clear, train people on their responsibilities and respond appropriately to complaints or suspected contamination. A strong quality culture is built through consistent practice, traceable decisions and a willingness to correct problems.
