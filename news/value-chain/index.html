Why the agrifood value chain is about more than growing food

Food moves through connected stages, from inputs and production to handling, transport, processing, retail and consumption. Gaps between stages can affect quality, costs and the price received by producers.

Food moves through connected stages, from inputs and production to handling, transport, processing, retail and consumption. Gaps between stages can affect quality, costs and the price received by producers.

Looking at the whole chain helps identify where value is lost or could be improved. Farmers may benefit from understanding buyer specifications and harvest timing; traders and processors may need more consistent volumes and handling; buyers need dependable quality and delivery. No single fix suits every crop or location, so practical decisions should start with local evidence, relationships and the costs each participant faces.
