How to participate

Stay informed and prepare to take part.

When an event is confirmed, review the announcement carefully and contact the organiser if you need clarification before registering or making travel plans.

Ask about upcoming activities

Contact Agrolyst with the event type or subject you are interested in and ask whether there are confirmed opportunities to participate.

Confirm the details

Check the date, time, venue or online access, intended audience, fees if any, and any registration requirements.

Register through the stated channel

Use the official registration instructions provided for the specific event and keep any confirmation you receive.
