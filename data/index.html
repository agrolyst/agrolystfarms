Reading data responsibly

Trust the process, not just the number.

Before using a statistic to make a business or production decision, check the context around it. Good data communication makes limitations visible rather than hiding them.

Know the source

Check who produced the information, how it was gathered and whether the source is appropriate for the question you are asking.

Check the date and location

Prices, yields, weather and availability change. Make sure the period and geographic coverage match your decision.

Understand limitations

Look for definitions, sample size, assumptions and gaps. A national average may not describe a particular farm or community.
