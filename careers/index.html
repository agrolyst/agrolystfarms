01 · Working with Agrolyst

Useful work begins with understanding real needs.

We value practical thinking, clear communication and respect for the people who grow, supply and use agricultural products.

Agriculture is a connected system. Farmers need access to appropriate inputs, relevant knowledge and reliable services. Customers need accurate information and responsive support. Suppliers and business partners need clear expectations and dependable communication. Helping those different needs connect takes people with different skills and perspectives.

Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services works across poultry and farm-related products, agricultural supplies, technical guidance, customer service and business relationships. The skills that support this work can range from hands-on agricultural experience to coordination, sales, communications, administration, digital tools and customer care.

We believe people can contribute in different ways. Experience matters, but so do reliability, a willingness to learn, sound judgement and the ability to work respectfully with others. A good fit depends on the real requirements of a role, the person's capabilities and the needs of the business at that time.

This page introduces the kinds of work that may be relevant to Agrolyst. It is not an advertisement for a specific position. When an approved vacancy is available, its duties, required qualifications, location, terms and application deadline should be clearly published before candidates are asked to apply.

What we value in a colleague

Whatever the area of work, we look for a thoughtful approach to doing the job well.

  • Integrity and dependable follow-through
  • Practical problem-solving
  • Respect for customers and colleagues
  • Willingness to learn and share knowledge
  • Clear, responsible communication
Vacancy status: This page does not announce an active vacancy or a closing date. Please contact Agrolyst to ask whether a role has been approved and is currently open. An expression of interest is not a formal job application.
01 · Free mentorship for corps members

NYSC corps members: learn, ask questions and grow with guidance.

Agrolyst Farms offers free mentorship to National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) members who want to build confidence, gain perspective and explore how their interests can connect with agriculture and enterprise.

Serving as a corps member is an important season for learning about work, service, people and the next stage of your career. Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services wants to help make that period more meaningful by offering mentorship at no mentorship fee. The aim is to share useful guidance, encourage thoughtful questions and help corps members think practically about their skills, goals and possible pathways.

Mentorship is a learning conversation, not a promise of employment, a paid placement or automatic admission into a role. It gives participants an opportunity to discuss their interests, learn more about an agricultural business and receive guidance relevant to the questions they bring. Any practical exposure, visit, task or additional activity must be discussed and confirmed with the Agrolyst team beforehand.

Corps members from different academic backgrounds can enquire. Agriculture is connected to many disciplines: business administration, accounting, marketing, communications, technology, logistics, customer relations, project coordination and data organisation can all contribute to how an agribusiness operates. Tell us what you studied, what you hope to learn and what kind of guidance would be most useful to you.

We encourage mentees to arrive with curiosity and a willingness to reflect. Bring questions about your career direction, skills you want to strengthen, challenges you are facing in your professional development, or how your academic knowledge may be useful in a real business setting. Mentorship works best when both sides communicate clearly and agree on realistic expectations.

What free mentorship means

There is no mentorship fee for the guidance described here. Participants should still confirm the format and arrangements directly with Agrolyst.

  • Career and professional-development conversations
  • Guidance on skills, confidence and workplace readiness
  • Opportunity to ask questions about agribusiness
  • Practical perspectives on teamwork and communication
  • Encouragement to set learning goals and follow through
MENTORSHIP FOCUS 01

Career direction

Talk through your interests, strengths, learning priorities and the kinds of experience you hope to build after or during service.

MENTORSHIP FOCUS 02

Professional skills

Explore habits that support reliability, communication, teamwork, initiative, planning and responsible problem-solving.

MENTORSHIP FOCUS 03

Agribusiness awareness

Learn about the relationships between farmers, products, customers, suppliers and the support services that help agriculture function.

MENTORSHIP FOCUS 04

Digital and business skills

Discuss how digital tools, clear records, customer communication and organised information support day-to-day work.

MENTORSHIP FOCUS 05

Workplace readiness

Build awareness of professional conduct, accountability, confidentiality, respectful communication and learning from feedback.

MENTORSHIP FOCUS 06

Questions and reflection

Bring a real question, reflect on the guidance shared and identify a practical next step you can work on yourself.

Important: Mentorship is free. The timing, format, mentor availability and any in-person activity must be confirmed directly. Do not travel to a location or assume a session is booked until the Agrolyst team confirms the arrangements.

02 · SIWES & Industrial Training

SIWES students are welcome at Agrolyst.

Agrolyst welcomes students who need practical workplace experience as part of the Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) or a related industrial training requirement. Students can enquire about suitable learning activities and the possibility of receiving guidance during their training, with arrangements confirmed in advance.

Are you a student looking for a place to complete your SIWES or industrial training? Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services welcomes students who want to learn, observe and contribute to relevant work in an agricultural business environment.

Industrial training helps students connect classroom learning with practical tasks, develop professional habits and better understand how an organisation works. We encourage students to approach their placement with curiosity, responsibility, a willingness to ask questions and respect for workplace guidance.

Depending on your course, learning objectives and the work suitable at the time, areas to discuss may include agriculture and farm operations, poultry and livestock-related activities, agricultural products and supplies, administration, record keeping, customer service, communications, business support or digital work.

Contact us before your proposed start date so the team can discuss your institution's requirements, training period, suitable activities, documentation and supervision arrangements.

Who can enquire?

Students who have a SIWES or industrial training requirement from their school, polytechnic, college or university.

  • Students who can provide an introduction or placement letter from their institution
  • Students with a defined training period and learning objectives
  • Students ready to follow workplace instructions and safety requirements
  • Students who can keep required records, logbooks or training reports
TRAINING AREA 01

Agriculture and farm operations

Where suitable to the course and available work, students may discuss farm routines, agricultural products, production practices, observation and basic record keeping.

TRAINING AREA 02

Business and administration

Potential learning tasks may include documentation, organising information, assisting with administrative routines and understanding business workflows.

TRAINING AREA 03

Digital and customer support

Where relevant and approved, students may learn about customer enquiries, communications, digital tools, data organisation and online business processes.

Before you begin: Please contact Agrolyst to agree placement details and confirm the required documents, supervisor, dates and suitable activities. Any allowance, transport or accommodation support should be discussed and confirmed directly; do not assume these are provided unless agreed.
03 · How to enquire

Share the details we need to guide you properly.

A clear first message helps the team understand your institution's requirements and the type of training you need.

When contacting Agrolyst about SIWES, introduce yourself and share your course of study, institution and proposed training dates. Tell us what you are expected to learn and whether your school has provided an official introduction letter, placement form or logbook.

You may be asked to provide relevant placement documents before a start date is confirmed. Send only information needed to arrange your training, and share sensitive personal documents only through a channel the company has confirmed is appropriate.

Details to include in your first message

  • Full name and active contact number
  • School or institution and course of study
  • Current level or year of study
  • Proposed start date and duration
  • Area of interest or required practical experience
  • Whether you have an introduction letter, SIWES form or logbook

05 · Our values

Trust grows through everyday behaviour: doing what you said you would do, asking questions early and treating people fairly.

VALUE 01

Integrity

Be honest about work, progress, limitations and commitments. Protect sensitive business and customer information.

VALUE 02

Clear communication

Share information at the right time, listen carefully and raise a problem before it becomes harder to resolve.

VALUE 03

Reliability

Respect agreed responsibilities and timelines, and communicate early when plans need to change.

VALUE 04

Continuous learning

Remain curious, improve skills and turn feedback and mistakes into practical lessons.

VALUE 05

Respect and teamwork

Recognise different experiences and perspectives, and help colleagues and customers feel heard.

VALUE 06

Practical problem-solving

Focus on the need, consider the available evidence and look for realistic, safe ways to improve the result.

08 · Frequently asked questions

Things to know before you get in touch.

We want career enquiries to be helpful and transparent about what is currently known.

Is mentorship free for NYSC corps members?

Yes. Agrolyst offers free mentorship to corps members. Contact the team with your background and the topics you hope to discuss. The format, timing and availability must be confirmed directly.

Can SIWES students enquire?

Yes. Agrolyst welcomes students looking for SIWES or industrial training. Please contact the team with your institution, course, required dates and documents so the placement arrangements can be discussed before you begin.

Do I need to pay for the mentorship?

No mentorship fee is charged for the corps-member mentorship described on this page. Ask the team to confirm the specific arrangements before taking part.

Are there any jobs open now?

This page does not announce an active vacancy. Please contact Agrolyst to ask whether any roles have been approved and are open for applications.

Can I send my CV for future consideration?

You may first ask the team whether it accepts expressions of interest or CVs for future consideration. Please do not assume that an unsolicited CV will be stored or reviewed as a formal application.

What areas of work can I ask about?

You can introduce your background in farming, poultry, agricultural supply, operations, administration, customer service, sales, communication, digital work or another relevant area.

Are salary, location or application deadlines available?

Those details must be confirmed for each actual vacancy. They are not provided on this general careers page because no specific opening is being advertised here.

Will contacting Agrolyst guarantee a job?

No. An enquiry only starts a conversation. Any recruitment decision depends on a real vacancy, its requirements and the company's process.

How can I avoid recruitment scams?

Use the contact details published on the official Agrolyst website. Be cautious of anyone demanding money, passwords, verification codes or sensitive financial details to secure employment.
