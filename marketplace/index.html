Frequently asked questions

Before you order.

Here are a few useful points to keep in mind when using the marketplace.

Where can I see current products and prices?

Use the product catalogue linked on this page. Current listing details depend on the products published in the live catalogue; contact Agrolyst if you need clarification.

Can I request a bulk quotation?

Yes, you can contact the team with the products, estimated quantities, timeline and delivery location you have in mind. Any quotation and terms need to be confirmed directly.

What if the item I need is not listed?

Send a sourcing enquiry describing the item or service. Agrolyst can review the request and tell you whether it can be supported; an enquiry does not guarantee availability.

Can Agrolyst help with equipment installation?

Agrolyst offers farm support and equipment-related services. Contact the team with details of your project to confirm the specific service, scope and availability.

How do I confirm delivery and payment details?

Confirm product availability, final price, delivery arrangements and payment instructions through official Agrolyst channels before completing a transaction.
