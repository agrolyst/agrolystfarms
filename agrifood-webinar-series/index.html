Why webinars?

Make room for practical knowledge and new perspectives.

Agriculture and food systems are shaped by many connected decisions: what gets produced, how it is handled, how businesses reach markets and how communities access food. Online conversations can help people share experience, ask questions and explore challenges across those connections.

What a useful session can offer

Each future session can be designed around a clear question and practical learning objective.

  • ✓Accessible discussion of a focused agrifood topic
  • ✓Shared experience from relevant practitioners and contributors
  • ✓Space for questions and constructive exchange
  • ✓Ideas participants can assess in their own context
Possible conversation themes

Topics across the agrifood value chain.

These are illustrative areas for future sessions, not a published webinar calendar. Specific topics, speakers and dates will be confirmed when available.

01

Farm production & resilience

Production planning, farm management, climate risks, input decisions and practical approaches to resilient operations.

02

Food processing & quality

Post-harvest handling, processing opportunities, storage, quality practices and reducing avoidable losses.

03

Markets & value chains

Buyer relationships, market information, logistics, pricing questions and ways to strengthen connections.

04

Agribusiness & finance

Business planning, costs, record keeping, financial readiness and responsible approaches to growth.

05

Technology & innovation

Digital tools, data use, practical innovation and the opportunities and limits of new approaches.

06

Food, people & sustainability

Food culture, consumer needs, livelihoods, inclusion and the relationships between agriculture and communities.
