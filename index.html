WHO WE ARE

Connecting farms, expertise and markets to grow the future.

Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services is a Nigerian agricultural solutions provider committed to strengthening the agricultural value chain through quality products, practical expertise, reliable services and strategic partnerships.

With a strong focus on poultry farming, we supply quality poultry feeds, poultry equipment and farm essentials, alongside consultancy, technical support and farmer education.

We believe agriculture goes beyond food production: it creates wealth, employment, enterprise and national prosperity.
