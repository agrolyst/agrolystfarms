Why Narrative Changers?

There’s more to agriculture than meets the eye.

Behind every harvest, food business and market connection are people making decisions, solving problems and learning as they go. Narrative Changers aims to make room for those experiences and ideas — from the farm gate to the wider food economy.

Conversations with a purpose

Our editorial direction is grounded in the realities and opportunities of agriculture and enterprise.

  • Practical perspectives: ideas that connect with real-world challenges and opportunities.
  • People and lived experience: room for farmers, founders, professionals, researchers and communities.
  • Constructive conversations: questions that encourage learning, collaboration and fresh thinking.
  • A wider food-system view: from production and processing to logistics, markets and consumers.

A few useful answers

Podcast FAQs

We’ll keep this page updated as confirmed information becomes available.

Where can I listen to episodes?

Episode links will be added to this page when episodes are released and their listening destinations are confirmed. No listening platform or episode catalogue is being advertised here yet.

When is the next episode?

A regular release schedule has not been announced. Please check back for confirmed episode announcements or contact Agrolyst.

Can I recommend a guest or topic?

Yes. Contact Agrolyst through the enquiry link and include the proposed guest or topic, a short explanation and relevant contact details.

Does it cost anything to be a guest?

No guest fee or commercial arrangement is stated on this page. Any proposed collaboration terms should be discussed and confirmed directly before making commitments.

Can my organisation partner with the podcast?

You can express interest in a collaboration. Partnership scope, editorial fit, deliverables, timing and any commercial terms would need to be reviewed and agreed directly.

Is this page a live episode player?

Not currently. This page introduces the series and explains how to make an enquiry; it does not yet host a live player or confirmed episode archive.
