01 · The owner

Leadership with a practical purpose.

A founder-led direction focused on useful solutions and long-term value across the agricultural value chain.

Had’j Femi Gafar is the Founder and Owner of Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services and serves as its Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer (MD/CEO). He provides strategic leadership and overall direction for the business, helping align its operations, customer relationships, partnerships and growth priorities with its purpose: to serve farmers and the wider agricultural community with practical, dependable solutions.

His role brings together business development and the day-to-day oversight required to keep a service-focused agricultural business moving forward. By maintaining attention on customer needs, supplier relationships, financial management and the development of useful agricultural services, he works to ensure Agrolyst remains responsive to the realities farmers face and the opportunities emerging across the sector.

Business direction

Setting priorities for the company, identifying opportunities for responsible growth and keeping the business focused on delivering value.

Growth and development

Exploring new markets, building relationships and strengthening the range of solutions available to customers and farming communities.

People and partnerships

Maintaining customer relationships, engaging suppliers and developing partnerships that support reliable agricultural service.

02 · Responsibilities

From strategy to everyday execution.

The responsibilities behind the founder’s role span the business, the customer experience and the company’s long-term direction.

Strategic leadership

Providing overall direction, defining priorities and guiding the business toward its goal of becoming a trusted provider of agricultural products and services.

Operations oversight

Overseeing daily business activity and helping ensure that customer needs, service delivery and internal priorities remain coordinated.

Financial management

Giving attention to financial planning, responsible resource use and the business decisions needed to support stable operations and sustainable growth.

Customer relationships

Listening to customers, understanding their needs and encouraging clear communication throughout enquiries, purchases and service engagements.

Supplier partnerships

Developing productive supplier relationships that can help the business serve customers consistently and strengthen access to relevant products and equipment.

Innovation and practical solutions

Encouraging the development of useful agricultural solutions that respond to real farming needs, changing market conditions and customer feedback.
