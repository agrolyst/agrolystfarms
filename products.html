OUR PRODUCT RANGES

Farm inputs and solutions for every stage.

Specific products and stock are managed in the live catalogue. The ranges below describe the types of solutions we support.

01 / POULTRY NUTRITION

Poultry Feeds

We distribute quality poultry feeds from trusted manufacturers, including Chikun and Ultima. Feed availability and specific formulations are confirmed against the live catalogue.

  • Chick mash and chick pellets
  • Grower mash and grower pellets
  • Layer mash and layer crumble
  • Broiler super starter, starter and finisher feeds
  • Selected premium formulations for specific production needs
02 / HOUSING & INFRASTRUCTURE

Poultry Cages & Housing Equipment

Equipment options to support efficient bird management, practical use of space and improved farm organisation.

  • A-type poultry cages
  • Brooding cages and battery cage systems
  • Cage feeding systems
  • Poultry house equipment and accessories
03 / DAILY FARM SYSTEMS

Feeding & Drinking Equipment

Practical equipment options to support routine poultry care, water delivery and feeding management.

  • Feeders and feeding troughs
  • Manual drinkers
  • Automatic drinking systems
  • Nipple drinker lines and accessories
  • Water storage and distribution components
04 / POULTRY STOCK

Day-Old Chicks & Poultry Birds

We support access to birds through appropriate sourcing and supply arrangements. Availability, breed, age, quantity and delivery are confirmed at the time of order.

  • Broiler chicks
  • Layer chicks and pullets
  • Point-of-lay birds
  • Finished broilers and market-ready poultry birds
05 / FARM OPERATIONS

Brooding & Farm Management Supplies

Selected supplies and accessories for poultry brooding and routine farm operations, including brooding equipment, poultry-house accessories and water-management components.

06 / FARM DEVELOPMENT

Farm Development & Equipment Solutions

We help farmers identify suitable equipment and farm inputs for their needs, including cage supply, equipment installation, feeding and watering systems, and planning support for functional poultry facilities.
