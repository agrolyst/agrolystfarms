OUR STORY

Growing productivity, enterprise and opportunity.

Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services is a Nigerian agricultural solutions provider committed to strengthening the agricultural value chain through quality products, practical expertise, reliable services and strategic partnerships.

We work at the intersection of farming, livestock production, agricultural inputs and market access, providing solutions that enable farmers and agribusinesses to operate more efficiently, improve productivity and build sustainable enterprises.

With a strong focus on poultry farming, we supply quality poultry feeds, poultry equipment and farming essentials while providing farm consultancy, technical support and farmer education. We also support poultry production through equipment installation, farm development guidance and practical management advice tailored to the needs of our customers.

At Agrolyst, we believe agriculture goes beyond food production. It is a platform for wealth creation, employment generation, enterprise development and national prosperity.
